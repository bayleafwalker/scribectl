# Tavi

## One-line function
The small passenger ferry that makes the short crossing of the inlet to the village landing.

## Ratified facts
- The Tavi carries passengers across the inlet; the road around it is longer.
- In season she approaches the village landing at 18:12 and 19:12 each evening.
- Her approach is heard as her engine and then her horn.
