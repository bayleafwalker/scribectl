# Café staff

## One-line function
The people working the café in the evening.

## Ratified facts
- They run the counter and the kitchen and take in deliveries.
- In the evening there are two of them: one at the counter, one in the kitchen.
- The café's ordinary supplies come across on the ferry; a delivery can also come round by road.
- They know what reaches the café: deliveries, notices, what customers say.
