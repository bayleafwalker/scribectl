# Landing

## One-line function
The village ferry landing on the sheltered side of the inlet, and one of the two listening positions.

## Ratified facts
- A sheltered landing on the inlet, with pilings and a boarding ramp that is lowered for each arrival.
- Audible here: wind, water against the pilings, ropes, the ferry's engine and horn, occasional footsteps, boarding-ramp sounds.
- Not audible here: the café and its conversations.
