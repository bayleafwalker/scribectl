# Café regulars

## One-line function
The people who are in the café most evenings, whom a regular listener comes to recognise.

## Ratified facts
- Two old friends keep the corner table most evenings. The older one is forever putting jobs off (the shed roof, the car that only plays up in the cold) and the younger one needles him about it.
- A woman who drives home round the inlet stops in most evenings for half a cup; she never stays long because of the drive.
- They talk about their own business: errands, family, jobs around the house.
