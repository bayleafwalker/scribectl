# Fragment 1.1815 — Café: Café

## Observation
Thursday, 18:05–18:15, inside the café.

## Brief
The café early in a cold Thursday evening. Render what the ear receives here, in order: the room, the radio under it, the road outside, whatever reaches it from across the water.

## Permissible speech act
Customers' remarks to each other about their own business. A passing word about the cold at most.

## Do not
- No news: nobody here discusses the ferry, the landing or anything they could not know.
- Sounds from the landing reach here only distantly and partly masked; its mechanical sounds cannot be heard at all.
- Nothing addressed to the listener.
