Fresh agent (Sonnet 5.5), given only `transcript-three-evenings.md`: fifteen
scripts in order (Thursday's café-staff fragment is the repaired draft-b),
no cards, packs, canon or event sheet. Report as returned:

1. Ordinary rhythm: the café is cups, spoons, a tap, the coffee machine hissing, low radio (guitar, accordion, a presenter half-heard), a car passing on the road every half minute, a door with wind, and two or three tables of small talk about shed roofs, jars and Monday. The landing is wind, rope creaking, water knocking, and on Thursday early evening a ferry arriving and leaving with a horn, ramp, footsteps and a few passengers. Café evenings wind down towards cups racked and chairs up (Saturday 20:10 closes the place).

2. What changed: the ferry stopped and a bus replaced it. Confidence: high on the change, medium on exact timing.
   - Thu 18:10: the ferry runs normally.
   - Thu 18:35: slush on the pilings, the ramp "does not settle", Leena says "Hm."
   - Thu 19:20: the radio announces tonight's last crossing is cancelled.
   - Thu 19:30: staff talk about an item running out ("last of it") and crates arriving "when it comes".
   - Fri 18:25: the radio says the ferry is closed because of ice and a replacement bus is running.
   - Fri 19:30: the bus stops at the café and passengers come in.
   - Fri 20:00: a delivery driver says "Long way round."
   - Sat: the bus is routine, and the landing is empty and iced.

3. Saturday felt like the same place. I recognised the spoon taps, the tap running, the accordion, "Aino" on the radio, the car passing and the bus pulling up. What I noticed myself was the landing: no horn, no engine, only slush and one lone walker. The café now has people with luggage ("Same as last night") and talk of timetables and the long way round.

4. Mostly yes. The empty Saturday landing made me want to go back to the Thursday ferry arrival, and Leena's ramp trying to settle in the slush. The Sat 19:50 talk about ferry-off travel, the bus timetable and Tuesday versus Wednesday pulled me towards the landing, wondering whether it was still iced and whether anyone had gone down there. The radio line "after the next one, the notices" and "tomorrow's notices" made me wonder whether the ferry would come back. It was a mild pull, not a strong one. Saturday was calm but not flat.

5. It felt mostly like overhearing, with a few explaining moments. The Friday radio notice is the most explanatory: "The Tavi's closed. There's early ice at the landing, and they say the ramp approach isn't safe." and "So that's the bus, not the boat, for now." The Thursday "That's tonight's final crossing, cancelled" repeats itself for the listener. Saturday's "I was going to go Tuesday, but with the ferry off it's the long way round anyway" also felt slightly handed to me, as did "It was on time today" and "Same as last night" (mild). The staff chatter about the board and the crates felt natural.

6. Inconsistencies: the ferry is called "the Tavi" only once, so I wasn't sure it was the same boat. Thursday 19:20 says "final crossing cancelled", but the 19:05 landing shows no ferry anyway. The Friday radio says the notice came "at midday", yet Thursday evening already had the ice and cancellation, which is slightly odd. Landing positions are few after Thursday. Friday's "Till eight" is stated, but the Friday 20:00 delivery is still going. The Saturday customers' "Wednesday I'm not going anywhere" has no earlier setup. The Thursday 19:30 "when it comes" has no clear referent.
