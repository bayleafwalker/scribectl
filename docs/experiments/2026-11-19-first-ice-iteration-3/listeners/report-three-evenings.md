# Blind listener — three evenings, both positions

Fresh agent (Sonnet 5.5), given only `transcript-three-evenings.md`: fifteen
clock-stamped scripts in order (Friday 20:00–20:10 is the repaired draft-b), no
cards, packs, canon or event sheet. Same questions as iteration 2. Report as
returned:

1. Ordinary rhythm: the café is a steady low hum. Cups on saucers, the coffee machine hissing, a tap or pan in the kitchen, and a car passing on the inlet road. The radio cycles accordion, then a mostly lost dedication, then guitar, then a short notice, then a fiddle tune. Two men at the corner table bicker about felt, the car, a spare room, a gutter, a ladder and ringing someone on Monday. Someone orders "just half" and leaves before it gets worse. The landing is wind, a creaking rope, slapping water, a strap that ticks, and a ferry docking around 18:13.

2. What changed: moderate-to-high confidence. Thursday 18:12 has a faint drone and a horn that nobody reacts to. At 18:35 the landing has slush, and the ramp will not settle at 18:43. At 19:20 the radio says the final crossing is cancelled. At 19:32 the café kitchen cuts the menu to soup and cheese sandwiches. On Friday the radio says the ferry is closed, with replacement transport and ice given as the reason. At 19:35 a bus brings passengers into the café, and a delivery arrives round the back at 20:05. On Saturday the bus is routine, the landing is empty, and a man says "With the boat off it's the bus for everyone anyway." The change is that the ferry has been replaced by a bus, and the bus has become habit.

3. Saturday felt like the same place. I recognised the half-cup woman, the two men, the radio's dedication-then-fiddle pattern, the bus at 19:35 and the soup, the car that "started Thursday... Second go", the felt, and "Monday". The spare room has been done, the café staff are the same, and the landing sounds as it did Friday. One thing I noticed unprompted: the radio is turned down to a talk programme at 19:50, which is not usual.

4. Saturday pulled my attention in a few places. The landing at 18:10 is empty, with only one brisk walker, and I wanted to know what the ferry's absence sounded like there. The notice paper in its frame at the landing is something I kept coming back to. At the café, the felt and the car callbacks sent me back to Thursday. The talk radio at 19:53 made me listen for what the voice was saying, which I couldn't make out. The 20:18 line "The presenter's voice starts, a few words, and is cut off with a click" also held my attention. The café ending at 20:19 is quiet and domestic, though "See you Monday" is nice.

5. Mostly overhearing. The three bulletins (19:20 Thursday, Friday 18:26, and the earlier ones) are the explaining parts:
   - "Right. This has only just come in, from the ferry."
   - "That's the notice as it reached us."
   - "The reason they give is early ice at the landing."
   - "That's what they've told us."

   These read as plot exposition inside a radio frame. Phrases like "their own business" and "Nobody looks up" are narrator-ish. Saturday's "With the boat off it's the bus for everyone anyway" is slightly on-the-nose.

6. Pacing: it felt like real time in the café on the long fragments (19:30 to 19:40 Saturday, 20:10 to 20:20 Saturday). The landing is the most patient. It rushed at the Thursday 19:20 bulletin, which is only two minutes and feels cut together. The jumps from 18:10 to 19:05 to 19:30 felt like highlights. Friday 20:00 to 20:10, the delivery, was a bit busy. The two-minute radio fragment and the five-minute Saturday 19:50 fragment felt montage-like.

7. Inconsistencies:
   - Saturday 19:50 has the radio as a talk programme, while the 19:30 Saturday radio has guitar and fiddle at the same hour range. The older man's "She started Thursday" is on Saturday, but Thursday's car started second go, which matches.
   - Thursday 19:05 café and Thursday 19:30 café overlap with the 19:20 radio fragment, so the Thursday timeline for the café has overlapping cues.
   - Saturday 19:30 the Younger Man says the boxes are in the car and Saturday 19:50 the older man worries about whether she'd start, which is a different car story from Saturday's "It started."
   - The Thursday 18:12 drone and horn are heard at the café while the landing horn is at 18:12:45, which is roughly consistent.
   - The woman with the cake on Saturday is "the half-cup woman" and says "Half, please", but Thursday she wanted the big pot back before Sunday, so that is consistent.
   - On Friday the older man says Monday and on Saturday he says "Monday, then. Or Tuesday.", which is consistent.
