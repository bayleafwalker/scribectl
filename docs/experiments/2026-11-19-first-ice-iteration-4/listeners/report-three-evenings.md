# Blind listener — three evenings, both positions

Fresh agent (Sonnet 5.5), given only `transcript-three-evenings.md`: fifteen
clock-stamped scripts from the final chain in order (Friday 20:00–20:10 is the
repaired draft-b), no cards, packs, canon or event sheet. Same questions as
iterations 2 and 3. Report as returned:

1. Rhythm: Café evenings are steady: coffee machine hiss, cups on saucers, two men at the corner table bickering gently (roof, car, ladder, wood), the radio with accordion/guitar/fiddle records and birthday dedications plus a short "notice" slot, a woman who comes in for "just half" and leaves, passing cars and vans, wind at the door. Landing: wind, water on pilings, rope creaks, chain knocks. At roughly 18:12 an engine and horn bring the ferry, ramp and a few passengers.

2. Change: it goes from open water to slush to ice to winter service. Thursday 18:10 is the ferry's last normal docking. At 18:43 Leena can't seat the ramp in slush. At 19:21 the radio announces the last crossing is cancelled. Friday 18:27 the radio says the ferry is closed because of ice and a replacement bus is running, and at 19:35 the bus stops at the café. Confidence high, for the ice and the move from ferry to bus. Saturday is the new normal: bus, no ferry. Smaller shifts: Thursday 19:33 the café is short on milk and bread (cartons, no bread, crackers). Friday 20:05 the delivery arrives and bread is back on the board.

3. Saturday was clearly the same place. I recognised the corner-table pair, "on the list" and the roof, "Half, thanks / Half it is", the radio dedications, "Go steady", the 19:35 bus stop with the stamping feet, the coffee machine, the frozen road. The difference I noticed unprompted: no ferry sounds, slush at the ramp, and a bus timetable on the sheet by the till. Nothing was narrated.

4. Yes, mildly. The "bus" and "sheet" talk at 19:51 sent me back to Friday's closure notice. Marjatta's grandchildren seeing her Sunday, and the "boat off" remark, made me think about the landing. The landing on Saturday at 18:10 is nearly empty: two people walk past and don't stop. It felt quiet and settled, not pulling. Saturday was not inert, but it works as a pleasant coda more than a hook. The café close at 20:15 felt like a natural end.

The jumps between windows (18:15 to 19:05, Thursday evening to Friday) feel like highlights, though in a believable way. Thursday 18:10 landing and Saturday 20:10 close ran slow in a good way. Friday 19:35 and Saturday 19:35 were busy. The Friday 19:35 and Saturday 19:35 bus sequences are near-identical, which made Saturday feel like a replay with small variations. A few of the stretches of radio notice chatter felt like set pieces.
